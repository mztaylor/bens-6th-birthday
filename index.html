How to link the pretty form straight to Google Sheets (Without iframes)

To have parents submit responses directly from this webpage into your Google Sheet without seeing the Google Form UI:

  1. Create your Google Form with the 5 questions listed earlier.
  2. Click the 3 dots in Google Forms > "Get pre-filled link".
  3. Fill in dummy answers ("name", "phone", etc.) and click Get Link.
  4. Inspect the URL to see the field keys, which look like entry.123456789=name.
  5. In this HTML file, in handleDirectRsvp(), send a simple fetch() POST to your Google Form's formResponse endpoint with mode: 'no-cors'.

Currently, submitted responses are safely stored in the browser's localStorage for immediate testing.
